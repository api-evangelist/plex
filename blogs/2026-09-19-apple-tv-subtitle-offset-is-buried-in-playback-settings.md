Player Version#: 2026.18.0 — Apple TV, tvOS 27.0 The new player UI has a dedicated subtitle menu, but it only lets you select or download subtitles. To adjust their timing, you have to leave that menu and navigate through Settings → Playback Settings → Subtitle Offset . Meanwhile, subtitle selection is also available in Settings.
